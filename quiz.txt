What is Side Channel Attack?
Side Channel Attack: A security exploit that uses information from a physical system to gain access to a system’s
program execution or functionality.
2
What are the common sources of Side Channel for Side Channel Attack?
• Measurable physical features
• Power consumption or current
• Timing or delay
• Electromagnetic radiation
• Optical
• Acoustic
• Output signals
3
What Is Modbus/TCP?
• An industrial protocol encapsulating the Modbus application layer inside TCP/IP
• Widely adopted for SCADA and PLC communication
4
What is Distributed Network Protocol 3 (DNP3)?
• DNP3 stands for Distributed Network Protocol version 3.
• Open standard protocol for SCADA and remote systems.
• Promotes interoperability across vendors.
• Common in Electric Utilities, Water, Oil, and Gas sectors.
5
What is Manufacturing Message Specification (MMS)?
An application-layer protocol for exchanging real-time data and supervisory control information between net-
worked industrial devices/applications.
6
What is MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport)?
MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport) is a lightweight communication protocol designed specifically for
resource-constrained IoT devices. It uses a publish/subscribe model, which allows for efficient and scalable message
exchange. MQTT is known for its low power consumption, minimal bandwidth usage, and reliable message delivery
through three levels of Quality of Service (QoS 0, 1, and 2). Due to its simplicity and efficiency, it is a suitable alternative
to traditional protocols like HTTP in IoT environments.
